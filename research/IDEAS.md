Capture is free. **Testing waits for Phase 3.**

One line per idea, dated. An idea here is not a commitment — it is a note so the thought isn't lost
and doesn't derail Phase 0 infrastructure work.

and the hardest question there is *"who pays me, and why haven't they stopped?"*
